On 29 Sep, all 53 small `Fix.txt` metadata files were SHA-256-verified against PhysioNet's official list in temporary scratch. [`bidmc_split_manifest.json`](../../ml/evaluation/bidmc_split_manifest.json) reserves 11 development, 15 validation and 27 held-out recordings by MIMIC matched waveform ID group; records 01/02 are development because they were previously inspected. The manifest contains no MIMIC IDs or demographics. There are 11/12/23 unique metadata-ID groups respectively, but patient independence still requires source/protocol review. Only the 11 development recordings' Signals/Breaths files were downloaded and SHA-256-verified; the other groups' waveforms/annotations were not examined. The versioned split reservation is **not** a frozen evaluation protocol.

The unchanged `bidmc-rr-baseline-0.1` development report [`bidmc_dev_baseline_0.1.json`](../../ml/evaluation/bidmc_dev_baseline_0.1.json) covers 11 hospital sensor recordings and 165 overlapping 60-second windows. All 165 were computationally estimated; there was no reference-missing or algorithm rejection. Recording-weighted mean MAE was **2.89** breaths/min versus annotator 1 and **2.76** versus annotator 2; worst recording MAE was **7.87** against either annotator. Mean signed bias was −1.57/−1.90 breaths/min. Mean absolute annotator disagreement averaged by recording was 0.39, with a worst recording of 3.13. Trailing `NaN` in unequal annotator columns was treated as padding after checking that all such entries were trailing; internal gaps are rejected. The method lacks a respiratory SQI, and these results do **not** establish acceptance yield, clinical accuracy or phone-camera validity (E-20260929-021/022).

The first two runs were inspected interactively, so both belong to development. Do not tune on validation or held-out windows after their results are viewed. A study lead must freeze preprocessing, gate, primary statistics and acceptance thresholds before final evaluation. Even all 53 sensor recordings would leave the Carda camera-domain comparison unresolved.
